Frequently Asked Questions

Answers for parents

Gifted Minds Scholars is a Pre-K through 5th grade tutoring program in Loganville, Georgia, led by Mrs. Wendy Bonnick. We offer in-person and online tutoring in reading, math, writing and academic support. Here are answers to the questions families ask most.

About the program

What is Gifted Minds Scholars?

Gifted Minds Scholars (GMS) is a tutoring program for children in Pre-K through 5th grade, based in Loganville, Georgia. It is an educational tutoring program of BACB21Co, founded and led by Mrs. Wendy Bonnick, who has more than {{years}} years of experience in education.

What grades do you tutor?

We tutor children from Pre-K through 5th grade.

What subjects do you offer?

We offer four programs: Reading & Literacy Development (phonics, sight words, fluency, vocabulary, comprehension and spelling), Mathematics Tutoring (number recognition through fractions, decimals and word problems), Writing & Language Arts (handwriting, grammar, punctuation, spelling, paragraph and creative writing), and Academic Support & Enrichment.

Who is the tutor?

Sessions are led by Mrs. Wendy Bonnick, founder and educational instructor of Gifted Minds Scholars. She has more than {{years}} years of experience in education and believes her gift for teaching was given from God. Her passion is helping each scholar discover their gifts, develop their skills and achieve greatness.

Learning needs and progress

Do you support children with dyslexia, ADHD or other learning differences?

Yes. Our Academic Support & Enrichment program provides individualized, multisensory and differentiated instruction and support for children with dyslexia, ADHD and other learning differences. If your child has an IEP, 504 plan or evaluation, sharing it helps sessions build on it.

Can you help a child who is behind in reading or math?

Yes. Reading and mathematics intervention is a core part of what we do. Sessions identify the skills your child is missing and rebuild them step by step, so they can catch up and gain confidence.

Do you work with children who are ahead and need a challenge?

Yes. Academic enrichment opportunities are available for children who are ready to go beyond grade-level work.

How will I know how my child is doing?

Parent communication and progress monitoring are part of every program, so you’ll know what your child is working on and how they’re growing.
