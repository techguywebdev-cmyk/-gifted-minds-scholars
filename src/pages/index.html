A learning journey by grade

The right support at every stage

Children need different things as they grow. Here’s what tutoring typically focuses on at each stage — always adjusted to your child.

  1. Pre-K – K

    Ready to learn

    Building the foundations that make school feel exciting instead of overwhelming.

    • Letter sounds & early phonics
    • First sight words
    • Number recognition & counting
    • Handwriting basics
  2. 1st – 2nd

    Building foundations

    Turning early skills into fluent reading, solid math facts and real sentences.

    • Decoding to fluent reading
    • Addition & subtraction
    • Place value
    • Sentence construction & spelling
  3. 3rd – 5th

    Growing independence

    Deeper comprehension, multi-step math and organized writing for upper elementary.

    • Comprehension & vocabulary
    • Multiplication, division & fractions
    • Word problems & decimals
    • Paragraph & creative writing

What grades does Gifted Minds Scholars tutor?

We tutor children from Pre-K through 5th grade in reading, math, writing and general academic support.

Yes. Our Academic Support & Enrichment program uses individualized, multisensory and differentiated strategies to support children with dyslexia, ADHD and other learning differences.
