A letter from Wendy

Continuing my beloved’s legacy

BACB21 was created to carry on the legacy of the late Brian A.C. Bonnick. My beloved son had a God-given talent to create, inspire, and motivate others. The brand, Perfectly Stated, was created by Brian, and the graphics were done by his best friend, Tatiyana “Perry” Terrell.

Our intention is to use this apparel to inspire young people and show them how they can walk in their purpose with God. Brian believed heavily in community outreach and the importance of networking. Therefore, most of the funds will be used to sponsor children who are less fortunate, to pay for books, lunch, and tuition for school.

Thank you for your support in continuing my beloved’s legacy.

Where your support goes

Sponsoring children who are less fortunate

Brian believed heavily in community outreach. Most of the funds raised through BACB21 and Perfectly Stated help pay for:

Books

Putting the right books in children’s hands so they can learn and grow.

Lunch

Making sure children have what they need to focus during the school day.

Tuition

Helping cover school tuition so more children can access a good education.

Carrying the mission forward

Every mind is gifted, and every child is an individual

Gifted Minds Scholars carries BACB21’s belief in individuality and self-expression into the classroom. Every scholar learns differently, so every session is built around the child: their strengths, their needs and the way they learn best.

Led by Brian’s mother, Mrs. Wendy Bonnick, Gifted Minds Scholars offers personalized Pre-K through 5th grade tutoring in reading, math and writing, in person in Loganville, Georgia and online.
