Carrying the mission forward

Every mind is gifted, and every child is an individual

Gifted Minds Scholars carries BACB21’s belief in individuality into the classroom. Every scholar learns differently, so every session is built around the child: their strengths, their needs and the way they learn best.

Led by Mrs. Wendy Bonnick, Gifted Minds Scholars offers personalized Pre-K through 5th grade tutoring in reading, math and writing, in person in Loganville, Georgia and online.
