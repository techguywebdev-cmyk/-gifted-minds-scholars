How does Scholar Zone help my child?

A few minutes of focused practice each day builds fluency and confidence. Scholar Zone gives your child three quick daily activities:

  • Sight-word flashcards from the Dolch lists for their grade
  • Math facts from counting to multiplication and division
  • A reading log with milestones like “25 books read!”
  • Stars, streaks and 16 badges to celebrate progress

Is my child’s information private?

Yes. Progress is saved only in this browser on this device. We only ask for a first name or nickname, and nothing is sent to us.
