Reading & Literacy Tutoring · Pre-K–5th Grade

Reading tutoring that builds confident, fluent readers

Gifted Minds Scholars offers reading and literacy tutoring for children in Pre-K through 5th grade, in person in Loganville, Georgia and online. Sessions cover phonics and decoding, sight-word recognition, reading fluency, vocabulary, comprehension and spelling.

What does reading & literacy tutoring cover?

  • Phonics & decoding
  • Sight-word recognition
  • Reading fluency
  • Vocabulary development
  • Reading comprehension
  • Spelling & word recognition

How do the sessions work?

Each session starts from where your child is right now. Mrs. Bonnick builds the foundations step by step — sounds, then words, then sentences and stories — and gives plenty of guided practice so reading starts to feel easy and enjoyable, not stressful.

Tutoring is available in person in Loganville, Georgia or online, and every session is scheduled by appointment. Parents receive regular communication and progress updates.

Could my child benefit?

Families often reach out when their child:

  • Has trouble sounding out new words
  • Reads slowly or guesses at words
  • Avoids reading or gets frustrated with books
  • Struggles to remember or explain what they read
  • Finds spelling especially hard
