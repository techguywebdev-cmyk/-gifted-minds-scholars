Personalized learning for every scholar

Gifted Minds Scholars offers four tutoring programs for children in Pre-K through 5th grade: Reading & Literacy, Mathematics, Writing & Language Arts, and Academic Support & Enrichment. Every program is available in person in Loganville, Georgia or online.
