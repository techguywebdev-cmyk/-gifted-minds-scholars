Mathematics Tutoring · Pre-K–5th Grade

Math tutoring that turns “I can’t” into “I’ve got this”

Gifted Minds Scholars offers math tutoring for children in Pre-K through 5th grade, in person in Loganville, Georgia and online. Sessions cover number recognition, addition and subtraction, multiplication and division, place value, fractions and decimals, word problems and problem-solving strategies.

What does mathematics tutoring cover?

  • Number recognition
  • Addition & subtraction
  • Multiplication & division
  • Place value
  • Fractions & decimals
  • Word problems
  • Problem-solving strategies

How do the sessions work?

Math builds on itself, so small gaps can grow quickly. Sessions find and fill those gaps, use hands-on and visual explanations so concepts make sense, and practice problem-solving until your child can explain their thinking with confidence.

Tutoring is available in person in Loganville, Georgia or online, and every session is scheduled by appointment. Parents receive regular communication and progress updates.

Could my child benefit?

Families often reach out when their child:

  • Gets anxious or upset about math homework
  • Hasn’t mastered basic facts for their grade
  • Struggles with word problems
  • Finds place value, fractions or decimals confusing
  • Can follow steps but doesn’t understand why
