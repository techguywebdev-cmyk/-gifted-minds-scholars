Writing & Language Arts Tutoring · Pre-K–5th Grade

Writing tutoring that helps children say what they mean

Gifted Minds Scholars offers writing and language arts tutoring for children in Pre-K through 5th grade, in person in Loganville, Georgia and online. Sessions cover handwriting, sentence construction, grammar and punctuation, spelling, paragraph writing, creative writing and written comprehension.

What does writing & language arts tutoring cover?

  • Handwriting
  • Sentence construction
  • Grammar & punctuation
  • Spelling
  • Paragraph writing
  • Creative writing
  • Written comprehension

How do the sessions work?

Good writing grows from clear thinking and solid mechanics. Sessions work on both — forming letters and sentences correctly, then organizing ideas into paragraphs and stories — so your child can write with clarity, accuracy and their own voice.

Tutoring is available in person in Loganville, Georgia or online, and every session is scheduled by appointment. Parents receive regular communication and progress updates.

Could my child benefit?

Families often reach out when their child:

  • Handwriting is hard to read or slow
  • Writes very little or avoids writing tasks
  • Sentences run on or lack punctuation
  • Has great ideas but struggles to get them on paper
  • Spelling errors make writing hard to follow
