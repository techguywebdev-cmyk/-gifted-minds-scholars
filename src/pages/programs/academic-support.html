Academic Support & Enrichment · Pre-K–5th Grade

Academic support for every kind of learner

Gifted Minds Scholars provides individualized academic support and enrichment for children in Pre-K through 5th grade, in person in Loganville, Georgia and online. This includes multisensory and differentiated instruction and support for children with dyslexia, ADHD and other learning differences.

What does academic support & enrichment include?

  • Individualized instruction
  • Support for diverse learning needs
  • Multisensory learning strategies
  • Differentiated instruction
  • Academic enrichment
  • Support for dyslexia, ADHD & other learning differences

How do the sessions work?

Every child learns differently. Sessions adapt the pace, format and approach to your child — using multisensory techniques, breaking skills into manageable steps and building in movement and variety where it helps. Children who are ready for more get enrichment that stretches and excites them. If your child has an IEP, 504 plan or evaluation, sharing it helps sessions build on it.

Tutoring is available in person in Loganville, Georgia or online, and every session is scheduled by appointment. Parents receive regular communication and progress updates.

Could my child benefit?

Families often reach out when their child:

  • Has a learning difference such as dyslexia or ADHD
  • Needs more individual attention than a classroom can give
  • Is falling behind and losing confidence
  • Learns best with hands-on, visual or multisensory methods
  • Is ready to be challenged beyond grade level
