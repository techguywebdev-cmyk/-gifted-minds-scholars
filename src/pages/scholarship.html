The Brian Albert Cornelius Bonnick Scholarship

The Brian Albert Cornelius Bonnick Scholarship is a one-time $1,000 award for tuition, books or other educational expenses. It is open to elementary, middle, high school and college students who meet one or more criteria for academic achievement, financial need, community involvement or special circumstances. Applications are due April 1, 2027 through bacb21.org.

About the fund

Carrying on Brian’s legacy

The Brian Albert Cornelius Bonnick Scholarship Fund was created to carry on the legacy of the late Brian A.C. Bonnick, who died suddenly at the age of 21. My beloved son had a God-given talent to create, inspire, and motivate others. Brian believed heavily in community outreach and the importance of networking. Therefore, this scholarship fund will be used to sponsor children who are less fortunate, to pay for books, lunch, and school tuition.

Thank you for your support in continuing my beloved’s legacy.

Wendy N. Bonnick (Mom)

Eligibility criteria

Who is eligible?

Applicants must meet one or more of the following criteria, and be in elementary, middle, high school or college.

Academic achievement

Minimum GPA of 3.5 on a 4.0 scale (or equivalent for international students).

Financial need

Documentation of household income, such as tax returns, FAFSA or other financial documents.

Community involvement

Demonstrated commitment to community service, with at least 30 hours of volunteer work in the past year.

Special circumstances

Consideration for students facing extraordinary personal challenges, such as medical conditions or family hardship.

Application process

What you’ll need to apply

Applicants are required to submit all of the following through the online application at bacb21.org.

Start your application
  1. Completed application formAvailable online at bacb21.org.
  2. Personal statement or essay500–700 words on personal growth, academic ambition or community impact.
  3. Unofficial transcriptsFrom your current school, showing academic standing.
  4. Letters of recommendationAt least two (up to three), from a teacher, counselor or community leader.
  5. Proof of financial needFor those applying based on financial circumstances, such as a tax report or other supporting documents.
  6. ResumeDetailing extracurricular activities, leadership roles and community service.

Questions

Scholarship FAQ

How much is the Brian A.C. Bonnick Scholarship?

It is a one-time award of $1,000, to be used for tuition, books or other educational expenses.

Who can apply?

Elementary, middle, high school and college students who meet one or more of the eligibility criteria: a minimum 3.5 GPA, documented financial need, at least 30 hours of community service in the past year, or special circumstances such as medical conditions or family hardship.

When is the deadline?

Applications open December 15, 2026 and must be submitted by April 1, 2027. Recipients are notified June 1, 2027, and the award ceremony is July 15, 2027.

How do I apply?

Submit the online application at bacb21.org/scholarship with your personal statement, unofficial transcripts, at least two letters of recommendation, your resume and, if applying based on financial need, proof of financial need.
