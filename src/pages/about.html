About Gifted Minds Scholars

Every child has a gift. Mrs. Bonnick helps them find it.

A calling, not just a career

“My gift for teaching was given from God.”

What guides our work

A nurturing place to learn and grow

Our goal is to provide a nurturing, encouraging and academically enriching environment where students develop confidence, strengthen essential skills and discover the joy of learning.

Confidence first

Children learn best when they feel safe and believed in. Encouragement and celebrating progress are part of every session.

Taught to the child

Instruction is individualized and differentiated, with multisensory strategies for children who learn differently.

Parents as partners

Regular communication and progress monitoring keep families informed and involved in their child’s growth.

About the program

Gifted Minds Scholars (GMS) is an educational tutoring program of BACB21Co, based in Loganville, Georgia. We work with children from Pre-K through 5th grade in four program areas:

  • Reading & literacy development
  • Mathematics tutoring
  • Writing & language arts
  • Academic support & enrichment

Families can choose in-person tutoring in our welcoming Loganville learning space or convenient online sessions from home. All sessions are scheduled by appointment.
